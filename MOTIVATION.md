# Why I created this project

I created this project just because I intend to hack a little with C. 
I figured out that the best way to do it is by creating an interesting, free, and useful software.

Hence, this project follows a purist way of doing things.
What do I mean?
It simply means that no AI tool or even a super-intelligent editor will be used in this project nor even consulted. My main capital is my head, my fingers, good health, a computer with the Debian OS in it, the GCC compiler, meson builder, and the vim editor.

Understand that my objective is not to ship out a product as fast as possible, but rather to experiment, learn, and have fun.

So, if you're like me or simply want to follow the progression of this software, follow me on github at https://github.com/ebongbilly or follow the progress through my blog at https://ebong-billy.site/category/free-software

# Why I created this project

I created this project in order to lead my students through the Senin path of C hackers. 
I figured out that the best way to do it is by creating an interesting, free, and useful software.

Hence, this project follows a purist way of doing things.
What do I mean?
It simply means that no AI tool or even a super-intelligent editor will be used in this project nor even consulted.
Our main capital is our head, fingers, good health, a computer, the GCC compiler, meson builder, and the vim editor.

Understand that our objective is not to ship out a product as fast as possible, but rather to experiment, learn, and have fun.
